contributors' Yoruba lexical entries, extracted by Wiktextract and published
through https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Yoruba/index.html.

Every sense retains a link to its source Wiktionary page and to that page's

the enwiktionary dump dated 2026-09-02. The pinned input SHA-256 is:

Changes made by Ede:
- Selected Yoruba common lexical parts of speech and single canonical headwords.
- Preserved source tone marks, glosses, parts of speech, labels and usage notes.
- Excluded proper names, characters, form-of/alternative-only senses,
  ambiguous canonical forms, quotations, examples with third-party references,
  examples without an explicit usage-example type, and nonlexical records.
- Reorganized eligible senses and selected up to five attributed usage examples
  per sense into Ede's JSONL and database representation.
- Added separate Ede editor-review status, initially source_imported.

The material is source-derived, not independently verified by Ede or a
credentialed native speaker. Editor review is not a licence or linguistic
accuracy guarantee. Dialect metadata remains unspecified where not established.
Raw source usage notes are preserved rather than converted into inferred facts.

This notice applies to the adapted dictionary snapshot and its displayed
definitions/examples, not automatically to Ede's application source code.
It does not clear the licences of older recovered corpus records, whose
unknown attribution/licence labels remain intact. Bulk corpus export remains disabled.

Wiktextract reference:
Tatu Ylonen, "Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data",
LREC 2022, pp. 1317–1325.

Sense-level synonym annotations from the same pinned source are retained as review proposals, with source qualifiers and attribution. Multiword annotations and self-links are excluded. No relation is automatically Ede-approved; approved links retain their source licence and attribution.
